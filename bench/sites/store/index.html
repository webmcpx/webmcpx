      About the studio

      Every piece is thrown by hand in our studio in Porto and fired twice. Glazes are lead-free and every piece is dishwasher safe unless the listing says otherwise. Because each piece is handmade, sizes and colors vary slightly.

      Shipping and returns

      Orders ship within 3 business days. Shipping is free on orders over $80; below that it costs $9. Returns are accepted within 30 days in the original packaging.
